How to Use

Guide to operations & features

Newly Added Numbers

Upload an Old File and a New File. The app extracts numbers that exist in the New File but are entirely missing from the Old File.

Extract Numbers from File

Upload one or multiple files at once. The app will scrub through them and extract every single valid number, combining them into one clean master list.

Check Availability

Cross-reference a 'Sorted' list against an 'All Numbers' database from your panel. It shows how many numbers are available in your panel and splits the results into what is Available and what is Missing.

Verify & Split

After processing, use the bottom buttons to verify your final results against an 'All Numbers' Excel sheet from your panel, or split massive text files into smaller chunks for further use.

Column Selection

The app will try to auto-detect the column containing your numbers. If the numbers aren't extracted automatically, manually select the correct column from the dropdown menu that appears after uploading your file.
